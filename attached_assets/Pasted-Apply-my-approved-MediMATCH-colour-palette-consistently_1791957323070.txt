COLOUR RULES
1. Keep navy and blue as the foundation for headers, navigation, headings, and links.
2. Use deep orange for primary actions such as Book appointment, Request consultation, Save, and Continue.
3. Use cyan for supporting highlights and selected decorative details.
4. Use green for successful, confirmed, and completed states.
5. Use red for errors, destructive actions, and urgent alerts.
6. Use yellow for pending states, reminders, and warnings, with dark text.
7. Use pale orange, green, red, and yellow tints on selected cards, icon backgrounds, and section accents.
8. Keep forms and data-heavy screens mostly white or softly tinted for readability.
9. Use the homepage’s multicolour accent strip where appropriate without putting every colour on every component.
10. Give the same action or status the same colour everywhere.

In operational screens, reserve status colours for their meaning. Decorative red must not make an ordinary booking card look like an error. Preserve any existing clinically meaningful colour conventions.
