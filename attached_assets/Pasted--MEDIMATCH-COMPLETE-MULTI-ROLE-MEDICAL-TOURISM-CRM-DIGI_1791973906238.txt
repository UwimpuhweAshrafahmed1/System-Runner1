# PHASE 8: MEDIMATCH COORDINATION BUSINESS MODEL

The most important business concept is that MediMATCH is an independent medical tourism coordination and clinic-matching platform.

MediMATCH is not automatically the provider performing treatment.

Patients contact MediMATCH to find appropriate healthcare providers in Istanbul.

The business process should operate as follows:

**STEP 1: Patient Inquiry**

Patient submits treatment, budget, travel dates, and preferences.

**STEP 2: Lead Creation**

A new lead is automatically created in the CRM.

**STEP 3: Clinic Matching**

The system identifies matching partner clinics.

**STEP 4: Clinic Comparison**

Patient receives clinic options within their budget and travel preferences.

**STEP 5: Clinic Selection**

Patient chooses their preferred provider.

**STEP 6: Consultation**

The patient is connected with an authorized medical professional for evaluation.

**STEP 7: Treatment Offer**

The clinic provides an authorized treatment quotation.

**STEP 8: Optional Coordination Services**

MediMATCH offers travel, accommodation, translation, concierge, and local assistance services.

**STEP 9: Booking**

All confirmed appointments, payments, and travel services appear in the system.

**STEP 10: Arrival and Treatment**

The coordinator manages arrival logistics and communication with service providers.

**STEP 11: Recovery and Follow-up**

The patient can contact the coordination team and access authorized postoperative instructions from their treating provider.

**STEP 12: Completion**

The patient can review services, receive receipts, and access relevant documents.

This entire journey must be connected across all four workspaces.

A single booking should be reflected appropriately in the CEO, Employee, Partner, and Client dashboards.
